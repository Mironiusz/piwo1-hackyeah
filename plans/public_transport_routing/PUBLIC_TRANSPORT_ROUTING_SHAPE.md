## Problem

Version 13 of `docs/product/specification.md` makes routes with the public transport of ZTP Kraków the optional feature O9, built first and in parallel with the mandatory features, within a time box of 4.5 hours of work of the people of the team. `plans_finished/valhalla_routing/` decided how such a route is computed (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` D-12) and handed the build to a separate initiative (D-13 there). `MVP.md`, section Initiatives, names this initiative for it. Nothing of O9 exists yet: the route operation of `docs/product/api_contract.md` knows only walking routes, no copy of the GTFS is fetched by anything, and no code of the backend exists.

The task prefix is `PUBLIC_TRANSPORT_ROUTING`, from the seed the user saved under the name of the initiative given in `MVP.md`.

## Recipient and trigger

- A person planning a route in Kraków who sets the switch of the route form to public transport. The request reaches the service through `plan_route` of `docs/product/api_contract.md` from the web frontend of `plans_finished/frontend_app/`, and possibly from the HarmonyOS client of `plans/stage5_harmonyos_port/`.

- A member of the team who fetches the copy of the GTFS before the demo and refreshes it by hand; nothing refreshes it on a schedule (specification, O9).

## Functional requirements

1. Kind of route. The contract carries the choice between a walking route and a route with public transport for the switch of the route form; walking is the default and one route is computed at a time. The switch itself is built by `plans_finished/frontend_app/` (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-5; seed).
2. Route with public transport. It uses the three feeds `GTFS_KRK_T`, `GTFS_KRK_A` and `GTFS_KRK_M`, departs now in the Europe/Warsaw zone, and its walking legs follow every rule of a walking route of M2, M7 and M8. It never boards or alights at a stop and never uses a trip the GTFS marks as not accessible; riding through such a stop on the same vehicle is allowed. Nothing of the request leaves the project (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-6; specification, O9).
3. Public transport segment. Green for the boarding, the ride and the alighting, whether the GTFS marks it as accessible or gives no accessibility information, with the source GTFS of ZTP Kraków and no date; not a fact, with no status, no vote and no flag. It shows the kind of vehicle, the line, the boarding and alighting stops and the departure time in hours and minutes, and the list of M8 holds it as an item in the order of the route (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-7; questions 2 and 3).
4. Public transport unavailable. When the route with public transport cannot be answered while walking routes can - no departure in time, the data with public transport unavailable, or no route with public transport that avoids every barrier and geozone its walking legs avoid - the service answers the walking route, the route with the fewest barriers when needed, with a plain statement that public transport was unavailable. When walking routes cannot be answered either, the answer is the plain message of M10 and no route (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-8 and FR-10; specification, O9 and M10).
5. Copy of the GTFS. A step of the loading program fetches the three feeds as a whole or not at all, before the demo and on a refresh by hand, never on a schedule, builds the routing data with public transport from the same copy of OpenStreetMap as the walking data, and keeps the last complete copy when a fresh one fails (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-9; question 5).
6. Days of the feeds. The service returns the day each feed of the copy in use was published, for the page about the data V-13 (question 6).
7. Contract first. Items 1, 3, 4 and 6 are written into `docs/product/api_contract.md` before the code that serves them, approved by Rafał in place of Marek and Adrian (seed; question 4).
8. Switch only where it works. The switch is shown only where a route with a tram in Kraków works; until then the app plans walking routes only (question 8).
9. Time box. 4.5 hours of work of the people of the team, counted from 04:32 on 2026-10-04 and recorded in the review; when they end without a route with a tram in Kraków, the feature is dropped, the switch is not shown, walking routes stay, and the work moves on to O1 (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-11; question 7).
10. Documents. Version 14 of the specification (questions 2, 3 and 8), the two registry entries moved to Resolved decisions (questions 1 and 2), and `MVP.md` and `docs/deployment/hosted_demo.md` following the parallel start and the step of the loading program (questions 1 and 5).

## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

1. Route with a tram (AC-8). Input: profile "I walk with a baby stroller", start Rondo Mogilskie, destination Tauron Arena, switch on public transport, a request at 09:30. Flow: the service plans a route departing now in Europe/Warsaw, with the walking legs checked against the barriers and geozones of the profile. State after: the walking legs carry their states of M7 and their barriers in the list of M8, and a barrier from the profile on the shortest walking way to the stop is avoided; the tram segment is green, with the source GTFS of ZTP Kraków, no date, the line, the two stops and the departure time, and offers no vote and no flag. With the switch untouched, the same request gives a walking route.
2. Stop and trip marked not accessible (AC-9). Input: a copy of the GTFS in which the stop where scenario 1 boards is marked not accessible, and one trip of the same line marked not accessible. State after: the route boards at another stop, does not use that trip, and may ride through the stop marked not accessible on the same vehicle.
3. No departure in time or no data (AC-10). Input: the switch on public transport at 02:30 with no departure soon, or with a copy of the GTFS that failed to load. State after: the walking route with its states and list, the route with the fewest barriers when needed, and a plain statement that public transport was unavailable.
4. No route with public transport around the barriers. Input: every route with public transport would cross a barrier or a geozone of the profile on a walking leg. State after: as scenario 3, never a route with public transport that crosses such a barrier.
5. Failed refresh of the GTFS (AC-11). Input: a refresh by hand in which one of the three feeds fails or is incomplete. State after: the last complete copy of all three feeds stays in use, routes with public transport keep working on it, and V-13 shows the days of the feeds of that copy.
6. Refresh of OpenStreetMap. Input: a refresh by hand of the copy of OpenStreetMap. Flow: the loading program builds the walking data and, in the step of this initiative, the data with public transport from the new copy, with the GTFS copy fetched in the same run or, when that fetch fails, the last complete one. State after: both kinds of route are computed from the same copy of OpenStreetMap, and its date is the one the route shows.
7. Engine not answering. Input: the routing service is down and the switch is on public transport. State after: the plain message of M10 and no route, walking or otherwise (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` AC-12).
8. Not ready at the check before the submission. Input: at 09:50 the time box has not ended, and a route with a tram does not work on the hosted demo. State after: the switch is not shown there, and the demo plans walking routes only (question 8).
9. Time box ended (AC-13). Input: the record in the review reaches 4.5 hours of work of the people of the team without a route with a tram in Kraków. State after: the switch is not shown anywhere, walking routes work, and the next optional feature started is O1.

## Notes on data, performance and security

- Personal data. A route with public transport is a route request of M2: it carries no identity of an account, its points and the profile are not stored or logged, and nothing of it leaves the project; the engine runs on the internal network of the demo (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` D-1, D-11). The choice of public transport adds nothing about the person. Fetching the GTFS is a request of the server of the project to ZTP Kraków that carries nothing of any route request.
- Time. Departure now is the moment of the request in Europe/Warsaw, and the departure time travels as an instant of the contract with the offset of Europe/Warsaw (`docs/product/api_contract.md`, section Conventions); the client converts no zone. The day a feed was published is a calendar day in Europe/Warsaw, like every day of the contract.
- Source of truth. The ways and the facts of the walking legs come from the copy of OpenStreetMap in use, and the stops, lines and timetable from the copy of the GTFS in use; a route never mixes the routing data of one copy of OpenStreetMap with another (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-3, and question 5).
- No writes by a person. The feature saves nothing a person sends, so it adds no idempotency key; the step of the loading program publishes a copy of the GTFS as a whole or not at all, so a repeated run never leaves a partial copy.
- Response time. A route with public transport on the scenario of AC-8 is answered within the 5 seconds a walking route has (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md` FR-4); when it falls back to the walking route, the call that answered no route adds at most its 2 seconds of `VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` D-10. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the person waits for one route at a time in both positions of the switch, and the spike measured 623 - 803 ms for a multimodal request with 2000 barriers (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` F-12).
- Memory and disk. About 700 MB of routing data with public transport and about 3.9 GB for its build, within the 16 GB of the server (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` D-14; `plans_finished/backend_architecture/BACKEND_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` D-11).
- Terms of use. The terms of use of the GTFS of ZTP Kraków were not checked (`VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md`, Risks), while the Kraków brief asks the team to state the terms of every source. Phase B or Rafał, for the submission, checks them; this shape does not assume them.
- Shared work with other initiatives. The step of item 6 waits for the form of a step `osm_import` writes down, and the routing data with public transport is built with the walking data of `osm_importer`, both owned by Mateusz; the walking legs and the fallback use the code of `route_planning`, owned by Marek. Each of these is agreed with its owner in phase B, never assumed.
- The number 14. Version 13 of the specification was already written twice on two branches and joined at a merge on 2026-10-04 (specification, section Decision provenance, entry Version 13). Other initiatives are shaped on the same tree at the same time, so the number of the version of item 5 is checked against the specification right before it is written.
