## 1. Purpose and the main scenario

One run shows the whole main scenario of the specification in Czyżyny, the district of the Tauron Arena, for a wheelchair user:

1. The person sets the needs with the preset "I use a wheelchair". Nothing about a disability is asked.
2. The person plans a walking route from the Tauron Arena to Ogród Doświadczeń by address search, the route of the `mobile_app/accessway/README.md` scenario.
3. The route shows its segment states, the summary and the list in three groups, with sample reports and geozones marked as sample data.
4. The person opens facts and reads their source, date and status: a sample user report and a fact from map data.
5. The person sees a high kerb report that map data contradicts, confirms it, and the route is planned again around that crossing.

The same run carries check 7.3: the contradiction of step 5, the stretches with no data or partial data, and, if time allows, a profile without barriers whose route is not assessed. Missing information is never shown as accessible.

Who uses this draft: Rafał for 7.2, 7.3 and 10.6; Mateusz for the sample data of check 3.3; Rafał and Adrian for the script of the video of check 10.2; Kuber for the data of `DemoSeed.ets`.

## 6. Known limitations to say during the demo

- Every report and area of the run is sample data, marked as such; the map data facts are real data of the copy, with its date.
- The demo and all its data are deleted on 4 October 2026, after the results are announced.

## 9. Discrepancies found

Between documents and code, found while writing this draft. None was changed.

1. `demo-kerb-medweckiego` in `DemoSeed.ets` has the street `Stanisława Lema`, while step 6 of the scenario in `mobile_app/accessway/README.md` places the fact "Wysoki krawężnik" at ul. Medweckiego. Real. The geozone `demo-flag-area` is the only fact of DemoSeed with the street `Medweckiego`.
2. The street name. `docs/product/views.md` decision 13 and `docs/product/user_journeys.md` decision 5 say the sample data of the demo carries the street, but `fact` in `docs/product/schema.md` has no street column and the Fact of `docs/product/api_contract.md` has no street field. The server sample data cannot carry it, so the web list shows no place line, while the video shows streets from `DemoSeed.ets`.
3. The HarmonyOS route result names the missing attributes of a stretch (`RouteView.ets`, function `gap`, "Brak danych: ..."), which M8 has not allowed since version 11. Already raised for Kuber in `plans/route_planning/ROUTE_PLANNING_SHAPE.md`; it matters here because the video now uses that client.
4. The local rules of the HarmonyOS app differ from the specification: the status is outdated when denials exceed confirmations by 2 (`Trust.ets`), not when denials reach 2 and outweigh confirmations, and it counts every person, not the latest five; the vote limit is 24 hours from the vote, not the calendar day of version 13; a contradiction is found within 15 m (`Facts.ets`), not 5 m on the same stretch, and only for kerbs, not for tag values such as the surface; outdated facts are left out of the map (`Facts.ets`, function `buildFacts`), while M4 keeps them with their status; an unverified report of a barrier is classed as information only (`EdgeRules.ets`, function `barrierIssue`), so whether its segment is in the state barrier, as M2 and AC-3 want for step 4, is to verify on the emulator. None of these should appear in the run, but the video should not show a case where they differ.
5. `mobile_app/accessway/README.md`, the docstrings of the domain code and the README's own scenario are in Polish, while the root `CLAUDE.md` and `mobile_app/CLAUDE.md` require English for everything in the repository.
6. Check 10.2 "on a running service" against the decision of Rafał of 04:40 to record on the emulator with on-device data (section 2, Q-1).
