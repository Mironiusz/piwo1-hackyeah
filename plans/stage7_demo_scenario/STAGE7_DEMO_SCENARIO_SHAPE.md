## Notes on data, performance and security

- No address, host, login or secret of the hosted demo enters any document this initiative writes (`CLAUDE.md`, section Target environment). The public link is typed by hand at the pitch, never written into the repository.
- Personal data: the run stores one vote with the one-way hash of the address and the browser of the presenter's laptop, kept until the demo is deleted on 4 October 2026 (M9); no account and no password of the demo is needed (question 4). The descriptions of the sample facts hold no personal data (Domain rules).
- Read visibility: every sample fact, the flagged S-6 and S-7 included, is public to anyone with the link, which is why the link is withheld until the pitch (question 6). S-8 is hidden and changes nothing.
- Source of truth: the content of the sample facts comes from `DemoSeed.ets` (question 2), their places and every fact of map data from the imported copy; nothing of the copy is assumed in the scenario before Mateusz verifies it (requirement 3).
- Time semantics, for `sample_data`: the dates of the sample votes are calendar days in the Europe/Warsaw zone (M4, version 13), shown as the date of the last confirmation; `DemoSeed.ets` places its votes 0.1 to 2 days before now, so on the server they fall on 2 - 4 October 2026. How the loading step writes them is the work of `plans/sample_data/` (draft Q-5).
- Idempotency, for `sample_data`: a second run of the loading step without emptying the database would double the sample facts and their votes; whether the step may run twice is the work of `plans/sample_data/` (draft Q-5).
- No new column, field or operation: the run uses only operations of `docs/product/api_contract.md` and columns of `docs/product/schema.md` that exist, and with step 8 out of the run (question 7) it does not depend on `not_assessed`.
- Query volume: one run makes two address searches and a few route requests; the address search of step 3 goes through the service to the geocoder of D-3 of `MVP.md`, so a rehearsal and the pitch add only a handful of requests.
