## Problem

Both challenges judge more than the running prototype. Kraków requires descriptions of the data sources with their terms, freshness, reliability and unavailability, of an architecture that separates the acquisition of data from its presentation, of the business model, of running the solution outside the infrastructure of the city with its costs, of data protection and of licences and dependencies, and a list of what works and what still needs work.
