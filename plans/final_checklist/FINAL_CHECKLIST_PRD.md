# PRD: Final checklist whose completion means the project is finished

Document state: 2026-10-04, approved by the user

## Business goal

The team of six people has about seven hours left to the Kraków deadline of 11:00 on 4 October 2026 and no product code yet. The team needs one list in the root of the repository that says when the project is finished, lets every person move their work forward at every moment of the day and shows which work can run in parallel, because what the team does today depends on it (seed). Next to the list, every piece of the work has an initiative with a stage that tells what it waits for, and every person knows what they carry.

## Problem and its consequences

- The work is spread over fourteen initiatives in `plans/`, the deliverables of two challenges, the open entries of `docs/standards/decision_registry.md` and a presentation that has no document. Nobody sees what is left, what can be dropped and what depends on what.
- `MVP.md` orders the initiatives by the code of other initiatives, so a person reading it waits for someone else's implementation. With seven hours left every such wait costs a deliverable.
- `MVP.md` gives Marek five initiatives and Kuba one, and the web frontend and the HarmonyOS port share the same two people. As long as the division is not written down, the team follows `MVP.md` and the load stays uneven.
- The clarifications, the run of the demo scenario, the HarmonyOS port, the documents the challenges require and the materials of the pitch have no initiative, so nobody owns them.
- Three documents contradict the decisions of the shape: `MVP.md` calls the Huawei submission optional, the registry still keeps the variant without a Huawei submission, and `docs/hackathon/challenge_requirements.md` says the official PDFs are not in the repository.

## Scope

- The list `FINAL_CHECKLIST.md` in the root of the repository, with the ten sets of the shape, the 42 checks of FR-5 and the stages of FR-6.
- Five new initiatives, each with its seed only: `stage1_clarifications`, `stage5_harmonyos_port`, `stage6_official_requirements`, `stage7_demo_scenario` and `stage8_materials_and_pitch`.
- A file `STAGE.md` in each of the twenty initiatives of `plans/`, this one included.
- `MVP.md` brought in line with the list, with the division of the work and with the mandatory Huawei submission.
- `TEAM.md` brought in line with the division of the work.
- `docs/hackathon/challenge_requirements.md` brought in line with the official PDFs in `docs/official/`.
- The entry HarmonyOS port and the Huawei submission of `docs/standards/decision_registry.md` brought in line with the mandatory Huawei submission.
- The documents that describe what an initiative holds, and `AI_WORKFLOW.md`, describing the new file `STAGE.md`, because a new file in every initiative changes the workflow (`CLAUDE.md`, section Task cycle and the agentic system).

## Out of scope

- Doing the work of the checks. The checks are carried out by their initiatives and by the people of `TEAM.md`.
- Shaping the five new initiatives. Each gets its seed here, and its owner takes it through the chain or works straight from the list (shape, requirement 9).
- Renaming an existing initiative or changing an existing seed, shape, PRD or plan.
- Owners, priorities and intermediate times in the list (shape, requirements 8 and 14). Owners go to `MVP.md` and `TEAM.md` (shape, requirement 22).
- Resolving a deferred entry of the registry: the start of O9, the wording of its public transport segment, the hash length, the stops of MSIP, the intellectual property and the licence. Those that block a check become checks of set 1.
- The optional features O1 - O8, everything after the pitch, the deletion of the hosted demo and its data, a licence file, and the decision whether the official PDFs are committed into the public repository.

## Functional requirements

FR-1. The list lives in the root of the repository as `FINAL_CHECKLIST.md`. It holds the ten sets of the shape, section Smallest meaningful scope, in that order, the checks of FR-5 and the stages of FR-6. The project is finished when the last check of set 10 is ticked (shape, requirement 6).

FR-2. Every check has four parts. What: the effect, with the initiative that delivers it. Done when: the effect a person verifies, which for a document means a person read it and found it complete against the requirement it serves; the artifacts of the chain are not a condition (shape, requirement 9). Waits for: the checks whose effect it needs before its own effect can be verified. Before that: what of the check can be done at once and which documents it starts from (shape, requirements 10 and 12). A check every part of which waits says so explicitly.

FR-3. The list is flat. No check names an owner, a priority or an intermediate time; the only times it names are the deadlines of the challenges. Check 4.2, O9, is the only one marked optional, and it does not condition the end of the project (shape, requirements 8, 14 and 15).

FR-4. Only Rafał ticks a check, after he has verified its effect (shape, requirement 17).

FR-5. The checks of the list are the following. A reference MVP AC-n is an acceptance criterion of `plans_finished/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, Valhalla AC-n one of `plans_finished/valhalla_routing/VALHALLA_ROUTING_PRD.md`, and D-n a technical decision of `MVP.md`.
