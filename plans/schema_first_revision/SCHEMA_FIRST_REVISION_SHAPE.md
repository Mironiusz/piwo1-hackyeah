- Did I read "only the tables" right? Not at first: I asked about the code of the rules, and Kuba answered with the task as Kuba sees it, a minimal model as a library for the import and the backend. The follow-up with variants showed that "only the tables" limits the shared model, not the revision, which still creates every constraint the specification and `docs/standards/standard_database.md` require (Problem).
- Does every scenario of AC-1 - AC-12 that left this initiative still have an executor? Yes, each goes to the initiative of `MVP.md` that builds the code it runs (Out of scope). Their seeds do not name these scenarios, so until requirement 8 is written into `MVP.md` they are found only in this shape; that is why requirement 9 puts the documents first.
- Does the vote limit by calendar day change the result of a scenario the acceptance criteria quote? No: in scenarios 1 - 3 of `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_SHAPE.md` and scenario 3 of `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md` every repeated vote of one person falls on the same calendar day as the vote before it or days later, never across midnight within a day, so each is refused or accepted as before.
- What does the calendar day give up? A person can vote twice within minutes across midnight, and a vote repeated because its response was lost is accepted as a second vote when the retry crosses midnight. Neither adds weight, because only the latest vote of a person counts, but the sentence of `docs/product/api_contract.md` that such a repetition "is refused in the same way, so a vote never counts twice" holds only within one calendar day, so the version of requirement 7 rewrites it.
- Is "a library" a package of its own? Not necessarily: the import runs as `python -m worker import-osm` from the same image and repository as the backend (`MVP.md`, D-14), so one module both entry points import is enough for what Kuba asked. The form is a technical choice of phase B of `plan-prd`. The model and the identifier of the revision also answer what Q-2 of `plans/osm_importer/` on `md/fast-setup` asks of the owners of the schema.
- Which description of the tables wins when the model, the revision and `docs/product/schema.md` disagree? `docs/product/schema.md`, the target that the revision matches line by line; the model follows the stored data and a test checks it, so a drift shows as a failing test rather than a silent difference. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: it follows from `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PLAN.md` D-21 and from the specification prevailing over everything else (`CLAUDE.md`).

## Domain rules or explicit TODO

- The rules are those of `docs/product/specification.md` in force and of `docs/product/schema.md`, which is part of it, with the two changes below. A change of the target schema is a new version of the specification approved by the user (`plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PLAN.md` D-21).
- The vote limit of M4 counts calendar days in the Europe/Warsaw zone: a person votes on the same fact at most once per calendar day, a second vote on the same day is refused, and a vote on the next calendar day is accepted, whatever the hours between them; of the votes of one person on a fact only the latest counts, as before. Decided by Kuba on 2026-10-04 in this interview (question 3), who named the key as the fact, the hash and the date, against the window of a day from the instant of the previous vote, which version 10 of M4 states ("only once a day has passed since their previous vote on it"). It replaces the domain rule of `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PRD.md` that the day runs from the instant of the vote and not from calendar days, and the limit stops being the configuration value x.
- The index `IX_vote_cast_at_with_voter_hash` leaves the target schema. Decided by Kuba on 2026-10-04 in this interview (question 3): it served only the task that cleared the hash after 30 days, which version 9 removed.
- What these two changes bring into the new version of the specification follows from them. Agent decision at C:40, without asking, each a consequence of the decisions above:
  - M4 states the limit by calendar day in Europe/Warsaw, the zone of every date of M10.
  - `vote` loses `repeat_allowed_at` with its offset, `CK_vote_repeat_after_cast`, `EX_vote_account_limit` and `EX_vote_hash_limit`, and gains the uniqueness of the fact, the person and the calendar day of the vote, one for the votes with a hash and one for the votes with an account. Kuba named a primary key; whether the uniqueness is the primary key is decided when the version is written, because `voter_hash` is null on every vote with an account and a primary key cannot hold a null column.
  - The extension `btree_gist` leaves the target schema, because only the two exclusion constraints needed it (`plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PLAN.md` D-16).
  - In `docs/product/api_contract.md` the error `vote_too_soon` of `cast_vote` means that the person already voted on this fact on the same calendar day, and `repeat_allowed_at` is the start of the next calendar day in Europe/Warsaw. The code and the field stay, so `frontend_app` needs no change of shape.
  - AC-6 of `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` - "within a day is refused; after a day it is accepted" - is read through the new version, since the specification prevails over that PRD (`plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, section Domain rules).

## Notes on data, performance and security

- The hash of a vote without an account is pseudonymized personal data and is kept until the demo is deleted (`docs/product/specification.md`, M9 and section Personal data). The vote limit by calendar day uses it as part of a uniqueness and keeps nothing more about the person; the tests use made-up hashes, never one computed from a real address.
- Time: every instant stays a pair of `timestamptz(3)` and its offset (`docs/standards/standard_time.md`), and the calendar day of a vote is the day of its instant in Europe/Warsaw, whatever offset the pair carries. A critical test proves the round trip of an offset (`plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PLAN.md` D-21).
- Idempotency: the stored data refuses a second fact with an idempotency key already saved and a second vote of one person on one fact on the same calendar day; a vote retried across midnight is the one known repetition it accepts (Challenging own assumptions).
- Rights: the service account gets exactly the rights of `docs/product/schema.md` and no right to create, alter or drop; a table whose grant is forgotten passes the migration and fails only in the service, so the rights are proven by critical tests (`agent_docs/memory/_cross_cutting.md`, entry What the local database setup creates and what only a revision creates).
- Volume: the revision reads nothing on a list path; the votes of the demo are few, and the facts and ways of the copy of Kraków are written by `osm_import`, not here.
- No address, account name or password of any environment enters the repository; the name of the service account in the grants is supplied when the revision is applied (`docs/standards/standard_config.md`, section Secrets).
