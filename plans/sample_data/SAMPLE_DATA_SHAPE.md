## Functional requirements

1. Supply the eight facts of the smallest scope in Czyżyny, with the content of `DemoSeed.ets` and the sample mark on every sample fact. Their source remains a user report; sample content never acquires the source OpenStreetMap. The descriptions are the Polish texts of `DemoSeed.ets`; the placeholders of S-6 and S-8 hold no real personal data.
2. Supply an actual contradiction between S-5 and a real lowered kerb of the imported copy under M2 and M4: the same stretch, no more than 5 m between the report and the kerb point. A missing tag never establishes a contradiction. If the selected copy cannot support it, loading must not claim that the contradiction was prepared.
3. Keep the sample mark available to the existing fact responses, so the frontend can display it on the map and list as FR-18 and AC-17 of `plans_finished/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` require. Implementing its visible presentation remains with `frontend_app`.
4. Load the samples as a required step of the manually invoked common loading program, after the OpenStreetMap data needed to validate the examples are available. A missing prerequisite or unsuccessful sample load is visible to that program.
5. Repeating the full loading flow must not duplicate sample reports, geozones or their sample votes, reset their existing community votes or moderation state, or change unrelated reports, votes and accounts. The retry behavior follows the user decision recorded by `osm_import`; its precise deduplication contract belongs to technical planning here.
6. Dates, author confirmations and reliability statuses follow M4 and M10. Each fact receives the sample votes that give its intended status in the table of the smallest scope, its first confirmation being the vote of its author; S-5 totals 1.5, so that one confirmation of the presenter without an account brings it to 2. The sample votes must leave the presenter eligible for that confirmation. No real identity or existing personal data may be used to fabricate sample authors. The user's Q-2 answer of one confirmation of weight 0.5 per example gives way to this rule by the decision recorded in Current state. Every sample vote is a fictional vote without an account, of weight 0.5, with its own fictional voter identity: each vote of weight 1 of `DemoSeed.ets` becomes two such votes, and S-5's 1.5 is three confirmations. No account is created or referenced (decided by the user on 2026-10-04, answering Q-3 with the first option). The creation of each fact and every sample vote are dated relative to the instant of the first successful loading, by the offsets of `DemoSeed.ets` of 0.1 to 2 days back, each stored with the Europe/Warsaw offset in force at its own instant; a fact is created together with its author's vote, and a repeated loading keeps the dates first written, so no sample vote ever lies in the future (decided by the user on 2026-10-04, answering Q-4 with the first option).
7. Set the moderation state of S-6 - S-8: S-6 and S-7 flagged, S-8 flagged and hidden. The loader, as part of the backend, writes `flagged_at` and `hidden_at` directly when it inserts these facts, dated in the same relative scheme as their votes; a hidden fact is always flagged as well. A repeated loading never resets a moderation state a moderator changed later (decided by the user on 2026-10-04, answering Q-5 with the first option).
8. Reserve the fact identifiers -1 - -8 for S-1 - S-8, in that order, reallocating -1 - -4, whose four-example content was never loaded anywhere. Record the reservation as a convention in `docs/product/schema.md`, section Facts, without a schema change: negative fact identifiers belong only to sample data, and the operations of `community_facts` and `community_facts_api` must accept them. ## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

1. First loading. Input: the current schema, the imported OpenStreetMap copy and the required sample dataset. Flow: the operator manually starts the common program, which validates the facts and loads their required sample records. Expected state: all eight facts are available, marked as samples, with the source and dates required by the specification, their votes and moderation state, and the intended statuses of the table of the smallest scope derived from those votes.
2. Contradictory data. Input: S-5 lies within 5 m of a real lowered kerb on the same stretch, with confirmation weight 1.5. Flow: the existing backend and frontend show it in the demo; the presenter confirms it once without an account. Expected state: before the vote the user report remains visible as unverified with its sample mark while the segment follows OpenStreetMap as M4 and M7 require; after the vote it reaches 2, becomes confirmed and the route is planned again around it. Those domain computations remain with their implementation initiatives.
3. Geozone. Input: S-4 is visible and not outdated or hidden, and the profile avoids poor surface. Flow: the existing routing operation evaluates a route through its radius. Expected state: the covered stretches are avoided under M2 and M5. A profile without that barrier does not acquire a new avoidance rule.
4. Repeat after partial loading. Input: samples were successfully loaded, a person later voted on or flagged a fact, and another step of the common flow failed. Flow: the operator fixes the failure and manually starts the complete program again. Expected state: the eight facts are not duplicated, no sample vote is counted twice, and the person's vote or moderation state and unrelated community contributions remain.
5. Invalid contradiction prerequisite. Input: the selected OpenStreetMap copy lacks the lowered kerb next to S-5, a required way, or the selected report is too far from the pedestrian stretch. Flow: the sample step checks the data. Expected state: the common program receives a visible failure rather than a claim that the demo contradiction is ready; no accessibility conclusion is invented.

## Challenging own assumptions

- Is a seed already an executable plan? No. The initiative has no agreed sample dataset or loading interface, so implementation cannot begin by guessing them.
- Can simplified OSM-shaped data from the mobile mock establish a real contradiction? No. They are synthetic geometry, and M2 requires evidence from the imported source copy. Since the reopening the content of the facts comes from `DemoSeed.ets`, but their places do not: the offsets of `DemoSeed.ets` from 50.0676, 19.9914 lie on a schematic network, so every place is chosen on real ways.
- Does mirroring `DemoSeed.ets` mean its weight of 1 can be copied as it stands? No. On the server a weight of 1 is a vote with an account, and a sample has no real account to cast it. The user decided in Q-3 to write two fictional votes without an account for each, which keeps every intended status and needs no account; at most four sample persons vote on one fact, so the window of five persons of M4 holds them together with the presenter.
- Do preloaded votes make the live vote of the presenter pointless? Not for S-5, which stops at 1.5. For the confirmed S-1 and S-3 a live vote changes nothing, which the scenario does not need.
- Does marking a fact as sample make it confirmed? No. Status is derived from votes under M4; the sample mark is independent of reliability.
- Can the sample loader erase and recreate its rows to avoid duplicates? That would lose user votes or moderation state and contradict the repeat-safe loading behavior already agreed in `osm_import`.
- Does stage 4 prevent preparation now? No. `MVP.md`, section Order and critical path, distinguishes starting from documents from verifying integration after prerequisite delivery.
- Can the loader claim success before the frontend displays the sample mark? Loading and frontend presentation have separate owners. Full AC-17 still needs their joint runtime verification.

## Notes on data, performance and security

The dataset has eight facts and their sample votes, not a new OpenStreetMap copy or a synthetic pedestrian network. The loader must preserve real contributions and avoid touching accounts or personal identifiers to generate sample votes. Sample descriptions contain no information about real people; the descriptions of S-6 and S-8 are placeholders that only imitate content a moderator would hide.

The sample mark does not grant an exception to the schema, voting rules or source provenance. Dates follow the existing Europe/Warsaw display rules and instant/offset storage contract. No hosting address, host, login or secret is recorded in the dataset or initiative artifacts.

Verification of the loading step requires the local schema and the delivery of the common program. End-to-end verification additionally requires the community-fact operations, route planning and frontend. Their absence limits the current evidence and must not be reported as a successful run.
