# Shape: Map tiles of the MVP

Document state: 2026-10-04, interview closed
Regulator: C:60

The seed carries no regulator value, so the interview ran at the default C:40 (`plans_finished/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-19). Adrian raised the value to C:60 at the gate of the PRD on 2026-10-04; it applies from phase B of `plan-prd`.

## Problem

The frontend draws one map of Kraków, and the browser may take its files only from the server of the project (`docs/standards/standard_frontend.md`, Rules that come from the product). Four things have to exist for that map: the tile archive, the style of the base map, the map fonts and the sprites. `MVP.md` gives them to this initiative, with the choice between committing the copies of the fonts and sprites and fetching them by a script, and the step of the loading program that puts the archive on the server of the demo (seed, the quoted row of `MVP.md`).

None of it is in the repository. `plans/frontend_app/` cannot draw its map without it, and on 2026-10-04 Adrian decided that the plan of that initiative waits for this one (`plans/frontend_app/FRONTEND_APP_PLAN.md` D-1).

## Recipient and trigger

- `plans/frontend_app/`, whose map component reads the archive, the style, the fonts and the sprites. The trigger is the map being drawn in a browser.
- The person who looks at the map: the streets, the parks and the water of Kraków with their names, in the language of the interface.
- The loading program of the hosted demo, built by `plans/osm_import/` and run by hand on the server, into which this initiative adds the step that loads the archive (`MVP.md`, Initiatives).
- The owners are Kuber and Adrian (`MVP.md`). Adrian builds the web frontend, this initiative included, and Kuber stays with the HarmonyOS port; the answer was given by Adrian alone (`plans/frontend_app/FRONTEND_APP_SHAPE.md`, Domain rules).

## Current state

- The technology is decided: one archive of vector tiles in the PMTiles format, cut from the daily Protomaps build of OpenStreetMap data for the bounding box of Kraków, zoom 0 to 15, never committed; the style from the Protomaps style package with the colors of the design direction and the labels in the language of the interface; fonts and sprites as copies served by the project; the attribution always open (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-5, D-6, D-7; `docs/standards/standard_frontend.md`, Technology).
- Adrian produced the files on 2026-10-03 and keeps them outside the repository: the archive cut from the build `20261003`, 34 785 215 bytes in 1295 tiles, carrying the OpenStreetMap state of 2026-10-03 04:00 UTC, and the fonts and sprites of the Protomaps assets, 776 files of 11.18 MB - three weights of Noto Sans in 256 ranges each and the sprites in a light and a dark variant.
- No style in the colors of the direction exists. The mocks of `.impeccable/briefs/views/` draw the base map by hand: a pale ground, white streets, pale blocks, a muted green for parks, and small gray street names.
- The repository has no `frontend/` directory yet; `plans/frontend_app/` creates the application there (`MVP.md`, Initiatives).
- On the server of the demo a reverse proxy serves the files of the frontend build and the tile archive, answering byte ranges, and the archive lies on a volume that the loading command of this initiative writes (`plans_finished/backend_architecture/BACKEND_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md`, lines 74 and 80).
- The loading program does not exist. `plans/osm_import/`, which builds it, holds only its seed and waits for `plans/schema_first_revision/` (`MVP.md`, Order and critical path).

## Smallest meaningful scope

The archive, the style and the fonts and sprites in a form the frontend can draw the map from, on a machine of the team and in the build of the application.

## Out of scope

- The map component of the application, its markers and the lines of a route: `plans/frontend_app/`.
- The facts and the routes shown on the map, and the copy of OpenStreetMap data behind them: the backend initiatives of `MVP.md`.
- The loading program itself and the proxy that serves the archive: `plans/osm_import/` and `plans/deployment_config/`.
- A date of the base map in the interface. The interface shows the date of the copy behind the routes only (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-7).
- Tiles generated by the team from the OpenStreetMap extract, and tiles of any outside service, both rejected in D-5 of that plan.
- The step of the loading program that puts the archive on the server. `MVP.md` gives it to this initiative, and the loading program it would be added to does not exist yet. Adrian decided on 2026-10-04, in phase B, in his words: "I will give the archive to the backend guys; until then we make our own temporary mock, then we switch over when it is ready; the point is to test it." The archive is therefore handed to the backend persons as a file, with the record by which it is checked, and they load it on the server; this initiative builds no step of that program. The backend persons have not confirmed it, and the row of this initiative in `MVP.md` is corrected with that caveat. Requirement 7 and scenarios 4 and 5 of the interview were removed with it; the answer of the interview that the server uses the file of Adrian stays.

## Functional requirements

1. The tile archive of Kraków exists as D-5 of the frontend stack plan describes, and the key of the build it was cut from is written down next to it.
2. The style of the base map shows the ground, the water, the parks, the buildings and the streets of Kraków with their names in the colors of the design direction, without icons of points of interest, and its labels follow the language of the interface, Polish or English. What the base map shows is an agent decision at C:40, without asking: it follows the mocks the user reviewed.
3. The fonts and the sprites the style needs are served by the project, and the style names no outside host.
4. The copies of the fonts and sprites are either committed or fetched by a script. The choice is made in this initiative and recorded.
5. The map shows the OpenStreetMap attribution stored in the archive.
6. A member of the team gets a working map on their machine from the written instructions, without help from the person who wrote them, and the instructions say how the archive file is handed to the persons who load it on the server and how they check it.

## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

1. A machine of the team. Input: the repository, the archive and the fonts and sprites obtained as the instructions say. Flow: the person starts the application. State after: the map of Kraków with its streets, parks, water and street names, and the OpenStreetMap attribution on it.
2. One host. Input: the map of scenario 1, moved and zoomed for a minute. State after: every request of the browser went to the host of the page, the requests for the archive in byte ranges included.
3. The language. Input: the map with the interface in Polish. Flow: the person switches to English. State after: a label that has an English name in the archive shows it, and every other label keeps its local name.

## Challenging own assumptions

- Can the whole chain of this initiative run before `plans/frontend_app/`, as its plan decides? The step of the loading program could not: it waited for the loading program of `plans/osm_import/`, which waits for the schema and the backend skeleton. Adrian first decided that `frontend_app` does not wait for that step, and in phase B took the step out of this task (Out of scope), so everything left here can run first.
- The style and a script are code, and code of the frontend lives in `frontend/`, which `plans/frontend_app/` creates. Running first, this initiative puts files into a directory the other one has not set up, without its gates. How the two fit is for phase B of both plans.
- Are the files of 2026-10-03 still the right ones on the day of the demo? D-5 asks for the newest build on the day the file is produced, which they were. Adrian decided on 2026-10-04 that the server uses the file he produced (Domain rules).
- Does the base map need the sprites at all? Sprites carry icons of points of interest and road shields. The mocks show none, so the style may need no sprite. Checked in phase B.

## Domain rules or explicit TODO

- The browser takes the archive, the style, the fonts and the sprites from the host of the page and from nowhere else (`docs/product/specification.md`, Personal data).
- The base map is a background. It is not a source of facts, carries no date in the interface, and the interface calls OpenStreetMap by name only in the attribution and on the page about the data (specification, M10).
- The archive is never committed (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-5).
- The archive of the hosted demo is the file Adrian produced on 2026-10-03 from the build `20261003`, handed to the backend persons as a file and not cut again on the server. Decided by Adrian on 2026-10-04, against a new cut from the Protomaps build made on the server and against the file with a cut as its fallback. The server therefore needs neither the cutting tool nor a connection to Protomaps.
- `plans/frontend_app/` waits for the archive, the style and the fonts and sprites, not for the step of the loading program. Until what it needs from a server exists, the frontend works on a temporary mock. Decided by Adrian on 2026-10-04, in his words "no, it is to work on a temporary mock", against the frontend waiting also for that step. Adrian also asked that the plan is followed without assuming when a part of another initiative will be ready, so this shape names what waits for what and no hour.

## Notes on data, performance and security

- Personal data: none. Reading the archive in byte ranges tells the server of the project which part of the map a browser shows; no other host learns it.
- Licences: the OpenStreetMap data of the archive under the ODbL, with the attribution on the map; the Protomaps style package under BSD-3-Clause; the fonts under the SIL Open Font License; the sprites derived from MIT licensed icons (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` F-11, F-12, F-13).
- Size: the archive is 34.8 MB and the full set of fonts and sprites 11.18 MB.
