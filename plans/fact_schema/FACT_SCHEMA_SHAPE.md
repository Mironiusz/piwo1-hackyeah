# Shape: Domain model and database schema of facts and votes for the MVP

Document state: 2026-10-03, interview closed
Regulator: C:40

The request carries no regulator value, so the default C:40 applies; the agent named it in question 1 of the seed and the user did not change it.

## Problem

Every requirement of the MVP that touches a barrier or an amenity - reports, confirmations and denials, statuses, geozones, the OpenStreetMap facts, the segment states, accounts and moderation - reads or writes the same data, but nothing says yet what that data is: which things exist, what identifies each of them, and which states and transitions they have. Without it the programming interface (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` Q-9) cannot name its resources and statuses, and the import, the route and the voting would each guess the shape of a fact on their own. `plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` names Q-10 the start of the critical path Q-10 -> Q-9.

- `docs/standards/standard_time.md`: every point in time is stored as an instant with the zone offset in force at the moment of writing.
- What the specification, version 3, says the data holds (`docs/product/specification.md`):
  - M3 - a user report is a point with a type from the closed list, an optional description and, for stairs, an optional number of steps; a saved report is not edited by anyone.
  - M4 - every fact, OpenStreetMap facts included, gets confirmations and denials; weights 1 for an account and 0.5 without one, the author of a report included; the statuses unverified, confirmed, disputed and outdated derived from the sums; one vote per fact per account or per hashed identifier; a status never changes with time alone.
  - M5 - a geozone is a point with a radius from a list and a barrier type, judged with the mechanism of M4, not edited after saving.
  - M6 - the date of the OpenStreetMap copy in use is shown.
  - M9 - an account is a pseudonym and a password; a vote without an account keeps a one-way hash of the IP address with browser characteristics, deleted after 30 days; deleting an account removes the account, the pseudonym and the points, while reports and votes stay detached and keep their weight; nobody else sees anything about the author.
  - M10 - every fact shows its source, the date it was obtained or last confirmed and its status; sample data is marked as such.
  - M11 - anyone can flag a report, a geozone or a photo; a moderator, assigned by hand, can hide it, and hidden content disappears for everyone.

## Functional requirements

The model has to hold what these requirements of `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` read and write:

1. FR-5 - a point report with its type, optional description and optional number of steps, never edited after saving, and the lookup of facts of the same type within about 15 m, OpenStreetMap facts included.
2. FR-6 and FR-7 - confirmations and denials of every fact with their weights, the vote limit of one person per fact and the window of the last k persons (question 5), and the statuses derived from them.
3. FR-8 - a geozone as a point with a radius and a barrier type, with votes and statuses like a point report.
4. FR-9 - the OpenStreetMap facts of the last fetched copy, and the date of that copy.
5. FR-10 and FR-11 - what is known about each barrier on each stretch of way, as `plans_finished/osm_barrier_mapping/OSM_BARRIER_MAPPING_PLAN.md` D-6 gives it, so that the route can derive the segment state and name the missing attributes.
6. FR-12 and FR-13 - accounts with a pseudonym and a password, contributions without an account with an identifier deleted after 30 days, and the detachment of contributions from a deleted account.
7. FR-14 - flags, the moderator role and hidden content.
8. FR-15 - the source, the date and the status of every fact, without anything about its author or the weights reaching other users.
9. FR-18 - the sample data mark.
10. The rules of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md`, now `docs/product/specification.md` version 3, M4: conversion, outdating with a reason, and the return of an OpenStreetMap fact with all its votes, in the reconciliation of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-10.
11. The first schema revision that creates this model, with its tests, in the form of `docs/standards/standard_database.md` (question 1).
12. The target schema written in `docs/product/schema.md`, which `docs/product/specification.md` declares part of itself, so that the revision is reviewed against it (question 2).

## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

The model has to be able to run these scenarios already decided elsewhere, without restating them: scenarios 3 and 5 of `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md` (status run, denying an OpenStreetMap fact) and scenarios 1 to 3 of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md` (conversion, outdating, return).

1. The identifiers of votes without an account expire. Input: stairs at G. Flow: 1 October an anonymous report of stairs at G (0.5); 3 October three anonymous confirmations (2.0); 3 October 12:00 the browser of one of them, on the same network, tries to confirm G again; 31 October the identifier of the report's author is deleted; 2 November the identifiers of the three confirmations are deleted. Expected state: on 3 October G is confirmed and avoided by a wheelchair route, and the repeated confirmation is refused; on 31 October and on 2 November G is still confirmed with 2.0 and avoided, no identifier of those votes exists any more, and the report still exists. The run was decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 3); its expected state from 31 October follows `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21, which the user chose on 2026-10-03 over the answer to question 3 (section Domain rules).
2. The identifiers of anonymous denials of an OpenStreetMap fact expire. Input: OpenStreetMap stairs at S. Flow: 1 October three anonymous denials (1.5) and one logged-in denial (2.5), and S is outdated; 31 October the identifiers of the three anonymous denials are deleted. Expected state on 31 October, with k of at least 4 (question 5): the denials still weigh 2.5, so S stays outdated. The run was decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 3); its expected state on 31 October follows `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21, as in scenario 1.
3. A person votes again and changes their mind. The values x = 7 days and k = 5 are illustrative only, the real ones are configuration (question 5). Input: stairs at F. Flow: 1 October 10:00 account U reports stairs at F (1); 1 October 10:01 U confirms F again; 2 October anonymous A confirms (1.5); 3 October account B confirms (2.5); 8 October 10:00 U, seeing a ramp instead, denies F; 9 October anonymous C and account D deny F; 10 October anonymous E denies F. Expected state: on 1 October 10:01 the second vote of U is refused, because x days have not passed, and F is unverified with 1; on 3 October F is confirmed; on 8 October only the latest vote of U counts, so F has 1.5 of confirmations (A, B) and 1 of denials (U) and is disputed; on 10 October the window holds the latest votes of the five persons who voted most recently - U, C, D, E as denials and B as a confirmation, while A has fallen out of the window - so the denials weigh 3, reach 2 and outweigh the confirmation of 1, and F is outdated. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 5 and its follow-ups); the status at each step follows the rules of `docs/product/specification.md` M4 applied to the window.

## Challenging own assumptions

- Do OpenStreetMap facts have to be stored as facts at all, or could they be read from the OpenStreetMap copy when needed? They have to be stored: they carry votes, and a fact that disappears and returns keeps those votes (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md`, scenario 3), so its identity has to outlive any single copy.
- Is a geozone a separate thing or a kind of fact? `docs/product/specification.md` M5 gives it a barrier type and judges it "with the mechanism of M4", so it has votes and statuses like a point report and differs in its area. Recorded without asking as an answer found in the specification; how it is stored is phase B.
- Is the author's report also a vote? M4 says the weights apply "the author of the report included", and scenario 3 of `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md` starts at 0.5 after an anonymous report alone, so a report carries the author's confirmation, and the author votes on it again only under the vote limit of question 5. Recorded without asking as an answer found in the specification and the scenario.
- Is Q-10 really independent of the routing engine (Q-1)? The facts are tied to OpenStreetMap elements, and the route needs to know which facts lie on its segments. That holds whatever the engine is, as long as the route can be related to OpenStreetMap ways or to positions; how is a question of `plans/routing_engine/`, not of this model. To be checked against that initiative when it closes, not assumed.
- Does the window of k persons break the conversion of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md`? No: the conversion compares confirmations and denials, and it now compares those in the window, the same sums the status uses. Its scenarios 1 and 3 keep their results as long as k is at least 3, because their votes come from different persons.
- Does deleting the identifier of a vote without an account change a status, against "A status does not change with time alone" (`docs/product/specification.md`, M4)? No: the vote keeps its weight after its identifier is deleted (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21), so no status changes by itself, and a barrier never becomes a confirmation of accessibility by the passing of time (M10).
- Is the status computed on every route a cost problem (blocking category query volume and cost)? The number of rows can be estimated: OpenStreetMap facts of Kraków in the order of tens of thousands, user facts in the demo in the order of tens, and at most k counted votes per fact. Whether statuses are derived on read or kept up to date on write is phase B, measured on the first import, not a product question. Agent decision at C:40, without asking.

## Domain rules or explicit TODO

- A vote without an account keeps its weight after its identifier is deleted 30 days after the vote was cast, for as long as the fact exists, in the sums of the status and of the conversion alike (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21). Votes of an account never expire either, also after the account is deleted (`docs/product/specification.md`, M9). A status therefore never changes with time alone (M4), and scenarios 1 and 2 keep their statuses after the identifiers are deleted. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03: question 3 was first answered with a vote deleted together with its identifier and counting for nothing, against keeping the weight of an expired vote; when the merge of the branch of this initiative showed that this contradicted D-21, decided in parallel in phase B of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/`, the user chose D-21, which replaces that answer.
- `docs/product/specification.md` version 3 does not state D-21 of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md`. This initiative proposes it for M9 together with its other changes to the specification (`plans/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PRD.md`, FR-14), while M4 ("A status does not change with time alone") and the section Out of scope ("Statuses that change with time alone") stay as they are. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: D-21 leaves the rule to a later version of the specification, and FR-14 is where this initiative proposes one; the approval stays with the user.
- Once the identifier of a vote without an account is deleted, nothing ties the vote to a person, so in the window it counts as the vote of a person of its own, like the vote of a deleted account, and the same browser on the same network can vote on the same fact again, both votes counting. For a person without an account the vote limit and the rule that only the latest vote counts therefore reach back at most 30 days. This is the effect question 4 accepts for a person who deletes an account and creates a new one. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: a consequence of D-21 of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` and of `docs/product/specification.md` M9; to be corrected by the user if wrong.
- A person - an account, or a hashed identifier for a vote without an account - can vote on a fact again once x days have passed since their previous vote on that fact; an earlier vote is refused. Of the votes of one person on a fact only the latest counts, so a person can change their mind or refresh a confirmation, but never adds weight by voting again. This replaces "One person has one vote per fact" of `docs/product/specification.md` M4. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 5 and its follow-ups), against a vote that is final, against counting every vote of a person in the window, and against keeping one changeable vote per account without a limit.
- Compatibility constraint from `plans/account_sessions/ACCOUNT_SESSIONS_PLAN.md` D-2: one vote per account and fact remains unique independently of the 30-day cross-mode hash. This conflicts with the x-day repeat-vote proposal above for account votes and with FR-7 of this initiative's PRD. The fact-schema owner is asked to reconcile this shape rule and PRD FR-7 with `docs/product/specification.md` M4 before schema implementation. This note records the requested action and does not change the decision on behalf of the owner.
- The status of a fact is derived from the latest votes of the k persons who voted on it most recently, with the weights and the thresholds of `docs/product/specification.md` M4 unchanged; older votes fall out of the window, so fresh votes decide. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (follow-ups to question 5).
- x and k are configuration values. The db person proposes them in phase A of the PRD of this initiative and the user approves them; until then the code works with default values held in configuration. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (follow-up to question 5), against fixing them in this shape. Two facts for the proposal, recorded without asking as arithmetic of the rules above: with k below 4, votes without an account alone can never reach the sum of 2 (3 x 0.5 = 1.5); and for a hashed identifier, an x of 30 days or more acts as 30, because the identifier is deleted after 30 days and the vote then belongs to no person (`docs/product/specification.md` M9, `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21).
- The window and the vote limit apply to every fact, OpenStreetMap facts and geozones included, because `docs/product/specification.md` M4 applies its votes to every fact and M5 judges geozones with the same mechanism. So the sum of 2 at which denials overturn an OpenStreetMap fact, and the comparison of confirmations and denials in the conversion of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md`, are counted in the window. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: a consequence of the rules above.
- The author of a report is a person like any other: the report carries the author's vote, and the author can vote on their own report again after x days, a denial included. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: a consequence of the rules above and of section Challenging own assumptions.
- A vote cannot be withdrawn without casting another; the user's rule names only a new vote. Agent decision at C:40, without asking, taken from the answer to question 5, which replaced the offered option of withdrawing.
- Earlier votes of a person and votes outside the window are kept as history, as `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md` keeps the votes of an outdated fact in its history; they only stop counting. The identifier of a vote without an account is still deleted after 30 days, while the vote stays (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21). Agent decision at C:40, without asking.
- This changes `docs/product/specification.md` in a later version, in M4 (one vote per fact) and in M9 (the identifier serves only to allow one vote per fact), and `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` in AC-6 ("A second confirmation of the same fact by the same account is refused" becomes refused within x days). AC-12 and scenarios 3 and 5 of `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md` keep their results as long as k is at least 4, because each of their votes comes from a different person. Recorded without asking as an answer found in the specification (question 4).
- The identity of an OpenStreetMap fact is the type of its OpenStreetMap element (node, way or relation), the identifier of that element and the fact type. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 6), accepting these effects for the MVP, against carrying votes to a new element by matching its geometry. The risk is negligible in the MVP, where the import runs by hand and once before the demo, and is described in the plan from prototype to service, next to the regular refresh of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_SHAPE.md`. Phase B of `plans_finished/osm_data_source/` settled the same TODO of that shape the same way in a parallel session (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-7); D-7 names the relation among the element types, because an amenity can sit on any element (`plans_finished/osm_barrier_mapping/OSM_BARRIER_MAPPING_PLAN.md` D-13), so the types here follow it.
- A hidden fact is out of everything for everyone: it is not on the map or the list, does not affect the route or a segment state, does not take part in the duplicate check within about 15 m, and cannot be voted on. Restoring widens M11 ("can hide it") and enters a later version of the specification with the other changes.

Agent decision at C:40, without asking: following M11 and keeping personal data to what a rule needs.

Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 (question 8), keeping scenario 3 of `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md` and AC-6 of `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, against the wording of `docs/product/specification.md` M4, which version 3 kept and a later version corrects. ## Notes on data, performance and security

- The identifier of a vote without an account is pseudonymized personal data, not anonymous data in the sense of the GDPR (`plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, Risks and notes).
- Deleting the identifiers of votes without an account after 30 days needs something that runs on time; when and how it runs is the worker of `plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` Q-11, not this model. Its delay changes no status, because the vote keeps its weight (`plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-21), but it keeps pseudonymized personal data longer than `docs/product/specification.md` M9 allows.
- One person with two accounts confirms a fact alone, because two logged-in votes reach the sum of 2 (question 4). The specification names no protection against this, and none is added here; it is a risk to name in the presentation, not a rule of this model.
- A person who sees that a barrier is gone shortly after confirming it cannot deny it until x days have passed; others can. This is the cost of the vote limit (question 5).
- The optional description of a report is free text that other users see, so it can carry personal data; moderation (M11) is the only control the specification names.
