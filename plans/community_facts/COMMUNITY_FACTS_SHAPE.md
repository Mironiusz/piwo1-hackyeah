# Shape: Data layer and status evaluator of the community facts

Document state: 2026-10-04, interview closed
Regulator: C:40

## Problem

The MVP has a decided product behavior for the community facts - point reports, geozones, confirmations and denials with their reliability statuses, votes without an account, flags and moderator hiding (`docs/product/specification.md` version 13, M3 - M5, M9 - M11) - a decided target schema for them (`docs/product/schema.md`) and a decided contract of nine operations (`docs/product/api_contract.md`, sections Facts and Moderation), but no code of any of it. Without it the main scenario stops at step 4: a person sees no status, no source and no date of a fact, cannot report a barrier or confirm one, and the Kraków demo cannot show the contradiction between OpenStreetMap and a user report that M10 requires.

Two other initiatives also wait for a piece of this work that is not an operation: the publication of a fresh OpenStreetMap copy of `osm_importer` calls "the same pure vote evaluator the backend uses" (`plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-8) and assumes that a vote locks its fact first (D-15 there), and the shared skeleton explicitly leaves both to this initiative (`plans_finished/backend_skeleton/BACKEND_SKELETON_SHAPE.md`, Out of scope).

## Recipient and trigger

- The web frontend of `frontend_app` and the HarmonyOS client of `stage5_harmonyos_port`, calling the nine operations of `docs/product/api_contract.md`, sections Facts and Moderation, on behalf of a person with or without an account, or of a moderator.
- The publication of a fresh OpenStreetMap copy of `osm_importer`, which consumes the status rules to decide the fate of a disappearing OpenStreetMap fact (M4) and runs concurrently with votes.
- `route_planning`, whose route facts carry the status, the source and the days of a fact (`docs/product/api_contract.md`, section Route, a route fact).
- `sample_data`, which writes sample reports and geozones with `is_sample` outside any operation (`MVP.md`, section Initiatives) and needs them read by the same rules.

## Smallest meaningful scope

Kuba chose on 2026-10-04, answering question 1, the part of the initiative that touches the database: the data layer of all nine operations and the status evaluator of M4. The smallest meaningful scope is that layer working against the local database of `db/`: every read and write the nine operations need, the vote write that a publication of a fresh OpenStreetMap copy can freeze, and the evaluator that derives the status of a fact from its stored votes, proven by tests on the stored data. It is verifiable without an endpoint, but it does not by itself tick checks 5.1 - 5.3 of `FINAL_CHECKLIST.md`, which are verified on the running service.

Kuba chose this over the vertical split the agent recommended - Kuba's votes, statuses, flags and moderation end to end, with the reports and geozones end to end with Marek - knowing that the API and service layers of the nine operations then need another executor.

## Functional requirements

Derived from the contract, the target schema and M3 - M5, M9 - M11 of the specification, each consumed by the service layer of the operation named; the wording of a requirement says what the stored data gives back, not how a response looks.

1. Facts in an area (`list_facts_in_area`). Read the facts whose point lies in a rectangle, at most 1000 and a sign that the rectangle holds more, leaving out hidden facts and the facts outdated because they were removed in OpenStreetMap (M4, M11).
2. One fact (`read_fact`). Read a fact by its identifier in any status; a hidden fact reads as not found, like a missing one (M11).
3. Facts near a point (`find_nearby_facts`). Read the facts of one type within 15 m of a point, nearest first, with their distance in whole metres, OpenStreetMap facts and outdated facts included, hidden ones and the facts outdated because they were removed in OpenStreetMap left out (M3, M11). Kuba chose on 2026-10-04, answering question 4, to leave out a removed fact, because its status stays outdated whatever its votes, so a person who took it for the barrier they see would confirm a fact that never comes back to the map or a route, and the barrier would be lost; a new report is visible instead. This narrows the sentence of M3 that an outdated fact takes part in the check, so requirement 9 carries it into the specification and the contract.
4. Saving a report or a geozone (`create_fact`). Save the fact and the confirmation of its author as its first vote together or not at all, once per idempotency key: a repeated save with the same key finds the fact of the first save and creates nothing, and the stored fact is what the caller compares the content of a repeated request with (`docs/product/schema.md`, `idempotency_key`; `docs/standards/standard_idempotency.md`).
5. Saving a vote (`cast_vote`). Save a confirmation or a denial of a person - an account, or the 32 bytes of the identifier of a person without an account the caller supplies - on a visible fact, refusing a second vote of the same person on the same fact on the same calendar day in Europe/Warsaw, with the start of the next calendar day as the instant the next vote is accepted, and refusing a vote on a hidden fact (M4, M9, M11). A retry never stores a second vote on the same day. The save locks its fact before it checks that the fact is visible and writes the vote, inside the transaction of its caller, so a vote on a fact a publication of a fresh OpenStreetMap copy is writing waits until that publication commits or rolls back, at most the 120 seconds of `plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-16, and is then checked against the published state. Agreed by Kuba on 2026-10-04, answering question 3, as D-15 of that plan assumes.
6. Flagging (`flag_fact`). Mark a fact as flagged at its first flag and keep that first instant, storing nothing about who flagged; refuse a fact from OpenStreetMap and treat a hidden fact as not found (M11).
7. Moderation (`list_flagged_facts`, `hide_fact`, `restore_fact`). Read every flagged fact, hidden ones included, the most recently flagged first, with the calendar day of its flag and whether it is hidden; hide and restore only a flagged fact, both repeatable without a change (M11).
8. Status evaluator. From the stored votes of a fact derive its status, the sums of confirmations and denials over the latest votes of the five persons who voted on it most recently, and the calendar day in Europe/Warsaw of its latest confirmation, by the rules of M4: the weights 1 and 0.5, only the latest vote of a person counting, a vote of a deleted account counting as a person of its own, and a fact removed in OpenStreetMap outdated whatever its votes. The same evaluator serves the operations, `route_planning` and the publication of `osm_importer` (`plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-8). Kuba chose on 2026-10-04, answering question 5, to deliver it first, before the data operations, with its interface agreed with Mateusz, as Q-1 of that plan asks, and with Marek for `route_planning`, so that both consume it as soon as it exists.
9. The rule of requirement 3 in the documents. A new version 14 of `docs/product/specification.md` says in M3 that a fact outdated because it was removed in OpenStreetMap takes no part in the check for existing facts, and `find_nearby_facts` of `docs/product/api_contract.md` says the same, before the data layer is built against it. Kuba approves the version; the change of the contract joins the confirmation of Kuber and Adrian still outstanding for D-12 of `MVP.md`.

## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

Every day is a calendar day in Europe/Warsaw in October 2026, every instant carries the offset of that zone, an account weighs 1 and a person without an account 0.5 (M4).

1. A report saved once. Input: the caller supplies an idempotency key K, a point report of stairs with 3 steps and the 32 bytes H1 of a person without an account. Flow: the report is saved, the response is lost, the same save with K arrives again. State after: one fact, `unverified`, with one vote - a confirmation of H1 without an account - and confirmations of 0.5; the second save finds that fact and stores neither a fact nor a vote.
2. Statuses of a report. Input: the report of scenario 1, saved on 1 October at 10:00. Flow and state after each step: 1 October 12:00 account A confirms - confirmations 1.5, `unverified`; 2 October 09:00 account B confirms - 2.5, `confirmed`; 2 October 18:00 account C denies - 2.5 against 1, `disputed`; 3 October 09:00 account D denies - 2.5 against 2, still `disputed`, because the denials do not outweigh; 3 October 12:00 account E denies - the five persons who voted most recently are A, B, C, D and E, H1 drops out of the window, 2.0 against 3.0, `outdated`. The day of the latest confirmation stays 2 October.
3. The daily limit. Input: account A and a fact F. Flow: A confirms F at `2026-10-04T23:59:59.000+02:00` - stored; A denies F at `2026-10-04T23:59:59.900+02:00` - refused, with `2026-10-05T00:00:00.000+02:00` as the instant the next vote is accepted; A denies F at `2026-10-05T00:00:00.500+02:00` - stored, and from then on only the denial of A counts; the same denial is repeated at `2026-10-05T00:00:03.000+02:00` because its response was lost - refused. State after: two votes of A on F, the latest a denial.
4. A vote during a publication. Input: an OpenStreetMap fact F with a confirmation of account A and a denial of account B, which a fresh copy no longer holds. Flow: at 03:00:00 the publication locks F and its votes and reads 1.0 against 1.0; at 03:00:02 account C confirms F and its write waits; at 03:00:40 the publication commits F as removed in OpenStreetMap, and the write of C continues, finds F visible and stores the confirmation. State after: F outdated with the reason that it was removed, off the map and the routes, the confirmation of C in its history; the conversion decision matches the history at the commit (question 3).
5. A removed fact and the check for existing facts. Input: the fact F of scenario 4, removed, and a person P who reports stairs 8 m from it on 4 October. Flow: the check for existing stairs within 15 m runs. State after: F is not among the facts found, P saves a new report, which is on the map as `unverified` (question 4).
6. Flags and moderation. Input: a report R, `confirmed`, and an OpenStreetMap fact O. Flow: on 3 October a person without an account flags R; on 4 October another person flags R again; a person flags O; a moderator reads the flagged facts, hides R, then restores it. State after each step: R flagged with the day 3 October and nothing about who flagged; the second flag changes nothing; the flag of O is refused; the moderator list holds R with 3 October, not hidden; while hidden, R is in no area, no check for existing facts and no reading of one fact, and a vote on it is refused as on a missing fact; after the restore R is everywhere again, `confirmed` by the votes it kept.
7. The sums for the publication. Input: two OpenStreetMap facts that a fresh copy no longer holds - G with a confirmation of account A and a denial of the person without an account H2, and J with a confirmation of account A and a denial of account B. Flow: the publication asks the evaluator for the sums of both. State after: G gives 1.0 against 0.5, so the publication converts it into a user fact; J gives 1.0 against 1.0, so it stays removed (M4).
8. A deleted account. Input: account A confirmed a fact F on 2 October and again on 3 October, and nobody else voted on F. Flow: F is read on 3 October - confirmations 1.0, `unverified`; A is deleted on 4 October; F is read again. State after: both votes stay with no person, each a person of its own (M4, M9), so F has confirmations of 2.0 and is `confirmed`.

## Challenging own assumptions

- Does "what touches the database" mean the data layer? It was the agent's first reading, but not the only one, so it was asked with four variants rather than assumed; Kuba chose the data layer of all nine operations with the status evaluator (question 1).
- Does the cut to the data layer break the guarantee D-15 of the importer plan needs? Not when the vote write is one data operation that locks the fact, checks its visibility and writes the vote, in that order, inside the transaction its caller opens. It still depends on the caller reading nothing it acts on before that operation and committing right after it; the executor of the service layer has to keep that, and the plan names it as a condition of the interface.
- Is the date of the latest confirmation taken from the counted votes or from the whole history? The contract says of a report that "a report carries the confirmation of its author, so it always has one", which holds only over the whole history, because the author can leave the window of five persons or later deny. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the latest confirmation in the whole history of the fact, which scenario 2 shows.
- Does a removed fact stay open to a vote? The contract refuses a vote only on a hidden fact, and after question 4 a removed fact is reachable only by its identifier. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the data layer stores such a vote, which changes nothing a person sees until a later copy brings the fact back, because changing it would change the contract without a reason found in a scenario.
- Does the repeat of a save whose fact has since been hidden reveal the hidden fact? The data layer gives the caller the stored fact with its hidden mark, and the contract already answers it: `fact_not_found` is among the codes every operation can return when "the fact is hidden and the operation is not a moderator one". Agent decision at C:40, without asking, as a consequence of that rule; the mapping is the service layer's.
- What does "the latest flag first" order by, when the stored data keeps only the first flag? The only stored instant of a flag is `flagged_at`, the first one, so the most recently flagged fact comes first and a repeated flag does not move it. Agent decision at C:40, without asking.
- Can deleting an account raise the status of a fact? Yes, as scenario 8 shows: two votes of one account on two days count as one person while it exists and as two persons after it is deleted. M4 and M9 say explicitly that each detached vote counts as a person of its own, and the specification prevails, so this shape builds it as written and reports the effect to the user rather than deciding against it.
- Is the evaluator a piece of the data layer? It is pure code over stored votes, which the importer plan calls the service-layer vote evaluator; Kuba took it into this shape by question 1, and which layer holds it is a question of phase B, not of the scope.
- Does the stage of `osm_importer` in `FINAL_CHECKLIST.md` reflect that it consumes the evaluator of this initiative? No: the list puts `osm_importer` at stage 2, waiting only for `schema_first_revision`, while its conversion of a disappearing fact calls the evaluator built here at stage 3. Delivering the evaluator first (question 5) lets the importer start; the list itself belongs to `plans/final_checklist/` and is reported to the user, not changed here.
- Is the volume of the read at every display of the map known? Estimated from the repository, see Notes on data, performance and security.

## Domain rules or explicit TODO

- Weights: an account 1, a person without an account 0.5, the author of a report included; a report carries the confirmation of its author as its first vote (M4).
- A person is an account or, without an account, its 32-byte identifier; a vote of a deleted account has no person and counts as a person of its own (M4, M9; `docs/product/schema.md`, Accounts and votes).
- Of the votes of one person on a fact only the latest counts; the status uses the latest votes of the five persons who voted most recently: outdated when the denials reach at least 2 and outweigh the confirmations, otherwise disputed when there are both, otherwise confirmed when the confirmations reach 2, otherwise unverified (M4).
- A fact removed in OpenStreetMap is outdated whatever its votes, and leaves the map, the routes and, by question 4, the check for existing facts (M4; `docs/product/schema.md`, Facts).
- A person votes on a fact at most once per calendar day in Europe/Warsaw; the next vote is accepted from the start of the next calendar day (M4; `UX_vote_account_day`, `UX_vote_hash_day`).
- A status never changes with time alone (M4; specification, Out of scope).
- A flag keeps nothing about who flagged and carries no reason; a fact from OpenStreetMap cannot be flagged; only flagged content is hidden; a hidden fact takes part in no operation that is not a moderator one and comes back with the votes it kept when restored (M11).
- No fact and no vote is ever deleted, and a saved fact is never edited (`docs/product/schema.md`, Who writes what).

## Notes on data, performance and security

- Query volume and cost, the blocking category for the read of `list_facts_in_area` at every display of the map, answered from the repository instead of asked: Kraków holds facts in the thousands - 5 324 nodes with `kerb` and 7 057 ways of steps among them (`plans_finished/osm_barrier_mapping/OSM_BARRIER_MAPPING_PLAN.md` F-19, F-22) - and the contract caps one rectangle at 1000 facts, while the votes exist only from the demo and its sample data, tens per fact at most. The status of up to 1000 facts is derived from their votes on every read (D-11 of `MVP.md`), so the votes of a rectangle are read together, never one query per fact. No time was measured; the plan measures it on the local database. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the estimate rests on the cited counts and the cap of the contract.
- Time: every instant is the pair of `docs/standards/standard_time.md` with the offset in force when it is written; `cast_on` is computed by the database in Europe/Warsaw whatever the offset; the start of the next calendar day carries its own offset, which changes on 25 October 2026 - after a vote on 24 October it is `2026-10-25T00:00:00.000+02:00`, after a vote on 25 October `2026-10-26T00:00:00.000+01:00`.
- Idempotency: a save of a report is identified by the SHA-256 of `create_fact:` and its key (`docs/product/schema.md`, `idempotency_key`); a vote has no key, and its retry on the same day is refused by the daily uniqueness, while a retry after midnight is a new vote that adds no weight (`docs/product/api_contract.md`, `cast_vote`).
- Personal data: the data layer stores the 32 bytes of a person without an account it is given and never derives, logs or returns them; no read of a fact returns its author, an account, a pseudonym, the identifier or the weights (AC-12 of `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PRD.md`). The sums of the evaluator are weights, so they serve the publication and `route_planning` and never reach a response.
- Read visibility: the moderator reads are the only ones that see hidden facts; whether the request is a moderator one is decided by `accounts` and the service layer, not by the data layer.
- Concurrency: a vote waits on a fact the publication holds (question 3) and holds only its own fact, so it adds no cycle to the order of `plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-15. A vote of an account deleted at the same moment either commits before the deletion and is detached by it, or finds no account and stores nothing, which the service layer answers as `session_expired`; the consumer scenario of AC-12 of `plans/accounts/ACCOUNTS_PRD.md`. Agent decision at C:40, without asking, as a consequence of the contract on a token of an account that no longer exists.
- Rights: the service account may select, insert and update `fact` and `vote` and delete neither (`docs/product/schema.md`, Rights of the service account), which matches a data layer that never deletes.
