## Decisions

D-1. One new module `data/community_facts.py` holds every new read and write; it reuses `StoredFact`, `StoredVote`, `VISIBLE_FACT_CONDITION` and `fetch_fact_votes` of `data/route_facts.py` and changes nothing there. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: F-8 shows the shared records and the vote read already exist, and the shared-helper rule forbids a copy.

D-2. The records of the contract, all frozen dataclasses in `data/community_facts.py`:

- `StoredCommunityFact(StoredFact)` adds `flagged_at: OffsetInstant | None` and `is_hidden: bool`; it carries no author, no account and no idempotency key, so `FactView` and `build_fact_body` take it unchanged.
- `StoredNearbyFact` holds `fact: StoredCommunityFact` and `distance_m: float`, the exact geodesic distance; rounding to whole metres is the service layer's.
- `FactContent` holds `fact_type: FactType`, `lat: float`, `lon: float`, `description: str | None`, `step_count: int | None` and `geozone_radius_m: int | None`, already normalized by the caller.
- `AccountVoter` holds `account_id: int`; `AnonymousVoter` holds `voter_hash: bytes` with `field(repr=False)`; `Voter = AccountVoter | AnonymousVoter`. The type makes a vote with neither person impossible, which the checks of F-5 would let through.
- `FactInsertOutcome` holds `fact: StoredCommunityFact` and `is_created: bool`.
- `StoredVoteInsert` holds `vote_id: int` and `cast_on: date`; `VoteDayTaken` holds `cast_on: date`, the day of the stored vote that refused the new one; `VoteInsertOutcome = StoredVoteInsert | VoteDayTaken`.
- `VoteAccountMissingError(Exception)` is raised when a vote names an account that no longer exists; its message carries no identifier.

Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the fields follow the contract Fact, the moderator item and the outcomes D-5, D-9 and D-10 of Marek's plan need (F-14, F-15).

D-3. The reads, each on the caller's connection, each returning a tuple of typed records:

- `fetch_stored_facts_in_area(connection, south: float, west: float, north: float, east: float, limit: int) -> tuple[StoredCommunityFact, ...]`: facts under `VISIBLE_FACT_CONDITION` whose point intersects `ST_MakeEnvelope(west, south, east, north, 4326)` compared as geometry, the edge included, ordered by identity, at most `limit`. The caller passes 1001 to learn truncation (F-14 S-3).
- `fetch_stored_fact(connection, fact_id: int) -> StoredCommunityFact | None`: one fact under `UNHIDDEN_FACT_CONDITION`, the hidden mark alone, in any status, a fact removed in OpenStreetMap included.
- `fetch_stored_nearby_facts(connection, fact_type: FactType, lat: float, lon: float, distance_m: float) -> tuple[StoredNearbyFact, ...]`: facts of the type under `VISIBLE_FACT_CONDITION` with `ST_DWithin` of the geography, the distance included, ordered by `ST_Distance` and then identity. The caller passes the 15 m of M3.
- `fetch_stored_flagged_facts(connection) -> tuple[StoredCommunityFact, ...]`: every fact with `flagged_at` set, hidden ones included, ordered by `flagged_at` descending and then identity descending.
- The votes of any read come from the existing `fetch_fact_votes` in one call for all returned facts (FR-4).

`UNHIDDEN_FACT_CONDITION` is a new constant of `data/community_facts.py`. The two predicates are two rules of the contract, not two copies of one: the map and the check hide removed facts, a reading by identifier does not; the critical tests run both sides (F-2). Agent decision at C:40, without asking: F-2, F-8 and F-15.

D-4. The area is compared as geometry, not geography. A geography envelope has geodesic edges that bulge north of the parallels in Kraków, so a point near the southern edge of the rectangle the client sends would fall outside it. The geometry comparison does not use `IX_fact_geog`; it scans facts in the thousands (F-27), which the critical test bounds by timing a read over 1001 seeded facts. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: correctness of the rectangle the contract describes ranks above performance, and no schema change is in scope.

D-5. The locks, each on the caller's connection, before any decision of the service layer:

- `fetch_stored_fact_for_vote(connection, fact_id: int) -> StoredCommunityFact | None` reads the fact with `FOR SHARE`, hidden or not, removed or not. `FOR SHARE` conflicts with the `FOR UPDATE` of the publication (F-11) and with the moderation lock below, so a vote waits for both, while two votes on one fact do not wait for each other.
- `fetch_stored_fact_for_change(connection, fact_id: int) -> StoredCommunityFact | None` reads the fact with `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` for a flag, a hide or a restore; it does not block the key-share lock a vote insert takes through `FK_vote_fact`.

A vote holds only its own fact and its new vote row, so it adds no cycle to the ascending lock order of the publication. The 120000 ms budget of the wait is set and restored by the caller around `fetch_stored_fact_for_vote` (F-14 D-12); this module sets no timeout. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the lock agreed in question 3 of the shape needs only to conflict with the publication, and the weakest such mode keeps votes on one fact independent.

D-6. `apply_fact_insert(connection, content: FactContent, idempotency_key: bytes, created_at: OffsetInstant, author: Voter) -> FactInsertOutcome` inserts a user report with `ON CONFLICT (idempotency_key) DO NOTHING`, the point built from `ST_SetSRID(ST_MakePoint(lon, lat), 4326)` with bound floats. When it inserted, it stores the confirmation of the author through `apply_vote_insert` at `created_at` and returns the new fact with `is_created` true. Otherwise it reads the fact stored under the key, hidden or not, and returns it with `is_created` false, storing no vote. A second save with the same key in another transaction waits on the unique index until the first ends, so the caller's transaction must be READ COMMITTED, the engine default, for the read after the conflict to see the committed fact. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: FR-5 and F-12; the comparison of content and the answer for a hidden fact are the service layer's (F-14 D-10).

D-7. `apply_vote_insert(connection, fact_id: int, verdict: VoteVerdict, voter: Voter, cast_at: OffsetInstant) -> VoteInsertOutcome` inserts with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, returning the identity and `cast_on`. Without a row it reads the `cast_on` of the latest vote of the same person on the fact by `cast_at` and identity, which is the vote that took the day, and returns `VoteDayTaken`. An `IntegrityError` on the constraint `fk_vote_account` becomes `VoteAccountMissingError`; any other error propagates. Either failure leaves the caller's transaction aborted, so the caller rolls back. The caller supplies `cast_at` from `fetch_business_now` after the lock (F-14 D-12, F-26). Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the database keeps the daily limit, and computing the day again in Python would be a second copy of the generated column.

D-8. The moderation writes return the fact after the write, read with `RETURNING`:

- `apply_fact_flag(connection, fact_id: int, flagged_at: OffsetInstant) -> StoredCommunityFact` sets `flagged_at` and its offset with `COALESCE`, so a repeated flag keeps the first instant;
- `apply_fact_hiding(connection, fact_id: int, hidden_at: OffsetInstant) -> StoredCommunityFact` sets `hidden_at` the same way;
- `apply_fact_restoration(connection, fact_id: int) -> StoredCommunityFact` clears both columns of `hidden_at`.

None of them checks the source, the flag or the hidden mark: the service layer decides on the state of D-5, and `CK_fact_hidden_only_flagged` remains the database's last guard. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: F-2 and the amended FR-8 and FR-9.

D-9. Nothing in this module logs, and no record exposes `voter_hash` or an account in its representation. The reads select no `account_id`, no `voter_hash` and no `idempotency_key` of a fact; the votes of `fetch_fact_votes` carry them only for the status rule (FR-10). Agent decision at C:40, without asking: AC-9 of the PRD and AC-12 of `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PRD.md`.

D-10. `schema_owner_engine` is delivered in `tests/conftest.py`, next to `database_cleanup_registry` that consumes it, so tests of every directory reach it: it reads `--scratch-database-url`, fails the test with a fixed message when the option is missing, builds the engine with `apply_engine_construction(make_url(address), SCRATCH_OWNER_STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_MS, True)`, yields it and disposes it. `SCRATCH_OWNER_STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_MS` moves from `tests/data/conftest.py` to `tests/conftest.py`, and `scratch_database` takes its owner from `schema_owner_engine` instead of building a second one. The collection guard of F-19 already refuses a nonlocal environment. Decided by Kuba on 2026-10-04 in phase B; the placement and the reuse by `scratch_database` are an Agent decision at C:40, without asking, from the shared-helper rule.

D-11. Critical tests follow F-12, F-16 and F-18. A case on one connection seeds and asserts inside `service_transaction`. A case that needs two connections commits invented rows: before the commit it registers with `database_cleanup_registry` an action that deletes, as the owner, the votes and then the facts with the registered idempotency keys or OpenStreetMap element identities, and accounts go through `stored_account_cleanup`. Invented OpenStreetMap identities start at 9300000001, apart from the 9200000001 range of `tests/data/test_route_facts_critical.py`. Agent decision at C:40, without asking: the service account cannot delete facts or votes (F-6).

The publication side of AC-3 runs the importer's own `fetch_osm_facts_for_update`, `fetch_osm_fact_history` and `apply_osm_fact_changes` on one connection, not the whole `apply_publication`, which needs the import exclusion and a workspace. ## Risks

R-1. The local database has never run on this machine (F-21); every critical result waits for the human steps, and no criterion is claimed before its run.

R-2. The area read scans every fact (D-4). With facts in the thousands it should take milliseconds, but nothing was measured; if the test bound of S-4 fails, a prefilter on `IX_fact_geog` around the rectangle is a change of this plan, not of the schema.

R-3. A caller that runs the insert of D-6 in REPEATABLE READ would read nothing after a conflict with a concurrent save; `data/DATA.md` states the requirement, and Marek's D-6 already writes in the engine default.

R-4. The vote day of D-7 is read as the latest vote of the person by `cast_at`; a clock running backwards between two votes of one person would name a wrong day. The service clock is the only writer of `cast_at`.

R-5. Moving the owner engine into `schema_owner_engine` changes `scratch_database` of the backend skeleton; its publication cases are rerun in S-4.

R-6. Deleting an account can raise the status of a fact, because its votes become persons of their own (shape, scenario 8); this follows M4 and M9 and is reported, not changed.
