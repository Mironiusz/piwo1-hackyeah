# MVP

Document state: 2026-10-04

## Why this document exists

This is the entry point to the MVP: what it covers, which technical decisions it rests on, which initiatives build it, who owns each of them, in what order they go, and which requirement of the MVP each of them meets. It was written on 2026-10-04 by `plans/mvp/`, which settled the MVP and handed every piece of its implementation to initiatives of their own (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-13, D-16).

It decides nothing of its own. `docs/product/specification.md` is the source of truth for the product and prevails over this file, `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` stays the contract the initiatives meet, and every item below points to the document that decides it.

A change that makes an item of this file untrue updates it in the same change. An initiative that was not finished when this file was written - `plans/schema_revision/`, `plans/valhalla_routing/` and `plans/deployment_config/` among them - adds what it settles here when it finishes, instead of writing into `plans/mvp/` (decided by the user on 2026-10-04, when `plans/mvp/` was carried out).

## Goal and deadline

By 11:00 on 4 October 2026 the team has a working prototype of the main scenario of the specification in Kraków that meets the requirements of the "Kraków bez barier" brief. The prototype is the core of the Kraków submission - the demo, the video and the presentation are built on it - and the base of a Huawei submission if the team goes for it (`plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, Business goal).

## Scope

- The mandatory features M1 - M11 of `docs/product/specification.md`, version 11, with the interface in Polish and English designed for a phone, and the privacy information inside the app of the section Personal data (`plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, Scope, FR-1 - FR-20).
- Area: routes in the whole of Kraków; the demo in Czyżyny, the district of the Tauron Arena, with sample data marked as such (`plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md`, FR-18 and Risks and notes).
- The optional feature O9, routes with public transport of ZTP Kraków, through its own initiative `plans/public_transport_routing/` and within its time box of 4.5 hours of work of the people of the team (specification, O9).
- Outside the MVP: the optional features O1 - O8, which get their own pass of `plan-prd` once M1 - M11 meet the acceptance criteria (`docs/standards/decision_registry.md`, entry Optional features of the MVP), and everything in the section Out of scope of the specification.

## How the MVP is built

`plans/mvp/` built nothing. On 2026-10-04 the user decided that it writes no product code: it settles the MVP, writes this file and sets up one initiative with its seed for every piece of the implementation (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-13, D-15). The layout recorded earlier in `plans/mvp/MVP_SHAPE.md`, one task for the whole MVP split into work packages, no longer holds, and `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` did not change.

Each initiative of the section Initiatives is a directory `plans/<name>/` holding its seed, and its owner takes it through the chain of `docs/standards/standard_agentic_workflow.md` - `plan-shape`, `plan-prd` and `plan-implement`. A seed carries no detail regulator, so each shape starts at C:40 unless its owner gives another value (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-19).

Where a decision below or a document it points to names a builder, the builder is the initiative of this file. Each operation of `docs/product/api_contract.md` is implemented by exactly one initiative, and an acceptance criterion of `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` is checked by the first initiative the section Requirements and initiatives lists for it.

Import ownership handoff on 2026-10-04: `osm_importer` now supplies the importer and Valhalla walking-data preparation. `osm_import` retains the copy-read operation and common demo-loading program. Shared backend and schema delivery stay with their existing initiatives (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-20). No seed is rewritten.

## Technical decisions

The numbers are those of `plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md`; D-13 is the layout of the section How the MVP is built.

- D-1. Backend: Python 3.13 with FastAPI, on PostgreSQL with PostGIS, with the Python profile of the standards kept in full. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 in phase B of `plans/mvp/`.
- D-2. Every other technical direction is decided in its own initiative by the role of the team responsible for it. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03 in phase B of `plans/mvp/`.
- D-3. Address search: the public Nominatim instance, called only by the server, with the text of the search only in the body of a POST request. Decided in `plans_finished/geocoding/GEOCODING_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-16, corrected by `plans_finished/nominatim_client/`.
- D-4. OpenStreetMap data: the Geofabrik extract of Małopolska cut to the boundary of Kraków, read with pyosmium, fetched once before the demo and refreshed only by hand; a fresh copy, its reconciliation with the stored facts and its date become visible together or not at all. Decided in `plans_finished/osm_data_source/OSM_DATA_SOURCE_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-22. Current timeout amendments are `plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-16 - D-18: 120 seconds for database publication, 60 minutes for the whole run, 30-second connection and read-inactivity timeouts and no separate 15-minute PBF download ceiling.
- D-5. OpenStreetMap tags to barriers and amenities: the rules of M6 - M8 of the specification, applied once per element, with the states present, absent, absent by default and unknown for every barrier of a way. Decided in `plans_finished/osm_barrier_mapping/OSM_BARRIER_MAPPING_PLAN.md` D-6 - D-17.
- D-6. Frontend: a single-page application in TypeScript with React, built by Vite into `frontend/`, with the map drawn by MapLibre GL JS from one archive of vector tiles of Kraków served by the project; the browser talks only to the server of the project. Decided in `plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-13.
- D-7. Local database: PostgreSQL 18.6 with PostGIS 3.6.4 and the files of pgRouting 4.0.1 in a project image built from `postgres:18.6-trixie` and started by Docker Compose, with a schema owner that applies the revisions and a service account that gets its rights only from them. Decided in `plans_finished/local_database/LOCAL_DATABASE_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-8.
- D-8. Account sessions: a signed token kept across browser closure, renewed on every authenticated request and expiring 24 hours after the latest one; passwords as Argon2id hashes; the moderator role checked on every moderator request; a request with an invalid or expired token is refused, and only a request without a token is anonymous. Decided in `plans_finished/account_sessions/ACCOUNT_SESSIONS_PLAN.md` D-3 and D-4, changed by `plans_finished/api_contract/API_CONTRACT_PLAN.md` D-5, D-6 and D-8.
- D-9. Routing engine: one Valhalla service run by the project from the image of `valhalla/Dockerfile` on the internal network of the demo, so nothing of a route request leaves the project; the route with the fewest barriers is computed on a graph of its own. Decided in `plans/valhalla_routing/VALHALLA_ROUTING_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-16, which replace part of `plans_finished/routing_engine/ROUTING_ENGINE_PLAN.md`.
- D-10. Where the demo runs: a server of the user in a data centre, with 16 GB of memory and 16 cores, reached at its IP address over plain HTTP, with every part of the demo in Docker containers started by Docker Compose. Decided in `plans_finished/deployment/`, superseding `plans_finished/demo_environment/DEMO_ENVIRONMENT_PLAN.md` D-8.
- D-11. Domain model and database schema of facts and votes: the target schema `docs/product/schema.md`, with the status of a fact derived from its votes on every read and the database refusing the states the rules forbid. Decided in `plans_finished/fact_schema/FACT_SCHEMA_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-21, with what the first schema revision has to meet carried in from FR-2 and FR-3 of `plans/schema_revision/SCHEMA_REVISION_PRD.md`.
- D-12. Programming interface contract: the sixteen operations of `docs/product/api_contract.md`, with the session token in the header `Authorization: Bearer` and renewed in `Session-Token`. Decided in `plans_finished/api_contract/API_CONTRACT_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-18.
- D-14. Backend architecture with the worker: one process started with `python -m api`, the import and refresh use `python -m worker.osm_import` locally or in a one-off container of the backend image, the copy the routing service serves read from `tileset_last_modified` of its `/status`, the backend image built on the image of `valhalla/Dockerfile`, every operation in the layers `api`, `service` and `data`, and no periodic task. Decided in `plans_finished/backend_architecture/BACKEND_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` D-1 - D-16. The command and execution locations follow the explicit later decisions of `plans/osm_importer/OSM_IMPORTER_PLAN.md` D-14 and D-20, recorded by `plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md` D-20.

## Order and critical path

1. `backend_skeleton` comes first and waits for nothing. `frontend_app` and `map_tiles` wait for nothing either and start at the same time; `frontend_app` builds against `docs/product/api_contract.md`, not against the code of another initiative.
2. After `backend_skeleton`: `address_search`, and `schema_first_revision` once the version of the specification with the idempotency key exists.
3. After `schema_first_revision`: `osm_importer` and `accounts`, then `community_facts` once the actor/moderator resolution of `accounts` exists. Marek supplies the Valhalla image/tools from `route_planning` before the importer builds its walking data; this prerequisite does not wait for route-operation integration.
4. After `osm_importer`: the route operations of `route_planning` and the copy-read/common-loader integration of `osm_import`. After that loader exists, `sample_data` and the loading step of `map_tiles` attach their steps.
5. After `route_planning` and `osm_importer`: `public_transport_routing`.

The critical path runs through `backend_skeleton` and `schema_first_revision`, because every backend initiative waits for one of them. The critical tests of `plans_finished/local_database/LOCAL_DATABASE_PLAN.md` D-8 that check the first revision stay with `schema_first_revision` and are never delivered by `backend_skeleton`, so that the loop cut by U-1 of `plans_finished/dependency_check/` does not come back (`plans/mvp/MVP_PLAN.md`, Risks).

## Known departures from the Kraków brief

- The hosted demo is served over plain HTTP, while the brief lists secure connections (D-10), so the start from the current location does not work on the hosted link.
- If O9 is built, a public transport segment without accessibility data in the GTFS of ZTP Kraków is shown as green (specification, M7 and O9).
- The Kraków submission states both as known limitations of the prototype (`docs/hackathon/challenge_requirements.md`, Conflicts and open points, item 10).
- Version 10 of the specification dropped from M10 the scene in which the demo shows routing that does not answer; the plain message of FR-17 of `plans/mvp/MVP_PRD.md` stays, checked outside the hosted link.
