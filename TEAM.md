## How documents name people

- A document written from 2026-10-04 on names a member of the team by first name, not by role: Kuba, not the db person; Marek, not the backend person.
- The frontend role is shared. A matter of the frontend names both Kuber and Adrian, unless only one of them decided or owns it.
- Role names stay where they name an area of work rather than a person, for example the frontend code unit.
- Documents written before 2026-10-04 keep their role names; this file is how they are read. Rafał decided on 2026-10-04 not to rewrite them.
