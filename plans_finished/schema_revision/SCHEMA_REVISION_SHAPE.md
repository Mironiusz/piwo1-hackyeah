## Scenarios: input, flow, expected state after the run

1. A save repeated after a lost response, the behavior requirement 1 brings into the target schema. Input: a person approves the summary of a stairs report at the Tauron Arena at 10:00:00, and the client generates the key K. Flow: at 10:00:01 `create_fact` with K saves the fact F1 with the confirmation of its author, and the response is lost; at 10:00:06 the client repeats the save with K and the same content; 25 hours later the same repetition arrives once more. State after: one fact F1, unverified, with 0.5 of confirmations; both repetitions return F1 with `200` and create nothing; the duplicate check within 15 m finds one fact, not two or three. The same K sent with another content is refused with `409 idempotency_key_reused` and creates nothing.

## Notes on data, performance and security

- The revisions are applied by the schema owner, and the service account gets only the grants a revision gives it (`plans_finished/local_database/LOCAL_DATABASE_PLAN.md` D-5).
