# PRD: Map tiles of the MVP

Document state: 2026-10-04

## Business goal

The map is where a person sees a route, its stretches, the barriers and the amenities, so the main scenario of the MVP cannot be shown without it. By 11:00 on 4 October 2026 the frontend draws the map of Kraków from files that the project itself serves, on a machine of the team and on the hosted demo (`MVP.md`, Goal and deadline).

Serving the map from the project is also what keeps the rule of the specification that no service outside the project learns the address of a person or the area they look at (`docs/product/specification.md`, Personal data).

## Problem and its consequences

- A map needs four things - the tile archive, the style of the base map, the map fonts and the sprites - and the repository holds none of them. The archive and the full set of fonts and sprites exist only on the machine of Adrian, who produced them on 2026-10-03.
- `plans_finished/frontend_app/` cannot draw its map without them, and its plan waits for this initiative (`plans_finished/frontend_app/FRONTEND_APP_PLAN.md` D-1). As long as they are missing, no screen with a map can be built.
- A style that pointed at the outside hosts its package names by default would send the address of every person to a third party.
- The archive is not committed, so nothing puts it on the server of the demo by itself. Without a step that does, the hosted demo has no map.

## Scope

- The tile archive of Kraków produced on 2026-10-03, with the record of the build it was cut from and of how it is checked.
- The style of the base map in the colors of the design direction, with labels in the language of the interface.
- The map fonts and the sprites the style needs, served by the project, with the decision whether their copies are committed or fetched by a script.
- Written instructions for getting a working map on a machine of the team and for handing the archive to the persons who load it on the server.

## Out of scope

- The map component of the application, its markers and the lines of a route. They belong to `plans_finished/frontend_app/`.
- The facts and the routes shown on the map and the copy of OpenStreetMap data behind them. They belong to the backend initiatives of `MVP.md`.
- The loading program itself and the server part that serves the archive. They belong to `plans/osm_import/` and `plans/deployment_config/`.
- The step of the loading program that puts the archive on the server. `MVP.md` gives it to this initiative, and the loading program it would be added to does not exist yet. Adrian decided on 2026-10-04, in phase B, in his words: "I will give the archive to the backend guys; until then we make our own temporary mock, then we switch over when it is ready; the point is to test it." The archive is therefore handed to the backend persons as a file, with the record by which it is checked, and they load it on the server; this initiative builds no step of that program. The backend persons have not confirmed it, and the row of this initiative in `MVP.md` is corrected with that caveat.
- A date of the base map in the interface. The interface shows only the date of the copy behind the routes (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-7).
- A new cut of the archive on the server. Adrian decided on 2026-10-04 that the server uses the file he produced.
- Tiles generated by the team and tiles of any outside service, both rejected in D-5 of the frontend stack plan.

## Functional requirements

FR-1. The archive. The tile archive of Kraków exists as D-5 of `plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` describes it, and a record next to it names the build it was cut from and a value by which a copy of the file is checked.

FR-2. The style. The base map shows the ground, the water, the parks, the buildings and the streets of Kraków with the names of the streets, in the colors of the design direction, without icons of points of interest. Its labels follow the language of the interface, Polish or English.

FR-3. One host. The fonts and the sprites the style needs are served by the project, and the style names no outside host.

FR-4. Committed or fetched. The copies of the fonts and sprites are either committed or fetched by a script. The choice is made in this initiative and recorded where the standard of the frontend leaves it open.

FR-5. The attribution. The map shows the OpenStreetMap attribution stored in the archive.

FR-6. Instructions. Written instructions take a member of the team from a clean copy of the repository to a working map, and say how the archive file is handed to the persons who load it on the server and how they check it.

## Acceptance criteria

AC-1 (FR-1). The archive covers the bounding box of the administrative boundary of Kraków at every zoom from 0 to 15. The record next to it names the build `20261003`, and a person who receives the file confirms with the recorded value that it is the same file.

AC-2 (FR-2). On the map of Czyżyny the streets, the parks, the water and the buildings are told apart, the names of the streets are readable, and no icon of a point of interest is drawn. The ground, the streets and the parks have the colors of the mocks of `.impeccable/briefs/views/`, and the names of the streets meet the contrast ratio of WCAG 2.2 level AA against the ground. With the interface in English a label that has an English name in the archive shows it, and every other label keeps its local name.

AC-3 (FR-3). While the map is moved and zoomed for a minute, every request of the browser goes to the host of the page. Read as a text, the style names no other host as the address of a file the browser loads; the one outside address in it is the link of the OpenStreetMap attribution, which a browser opens only when a person presses it.

AC-4 (FR-4). The decision stands in writing with its reason, and the repository holds either the copies or the script that fetches them, not both.

AC-5 (FR-5). The attribution to OpenStreetMap is visible on the map without any action of the person.

AC-6 (FR-6). A member of the team who follows the instructions on a clean copy of the repository sees the map of Kraków without asking their author. A person who receives the archive file finds in the instructions where the frontend expects it to be served and the value by which the file is checked.

## Domain rules

- The browser takes the archive, the style, the fonts and the sprites from the host of the page and from nowhere else (specification, Personal data).
- The base map is a background. It is not a source of facts and carries no date in the interface, and the interface names OpenStreetMap only in the attribution on the map and on the page about the data (specification, M10).
- The archive is never committed (`plans_finished/frontend_stack/FRONTEND_STACK_PLAN.md` D-5).
- The archive of the hosted demo is the file Adrian produced on 2026-10-03 from the build `20261003`. It is handed to the backend persons as a file and is not cut again on the server (`plans_finished/map_tiles/MAP_TILES_SHAPE.md`, Domain rules).
- What the base map shows follows the mocks the user reviewed: no icons of points of interest.
- `plans_finished/frontend_app/` waits for FR-1 - FR-6. Until the server serves the archive, the frontend takes it from a temporary stand-in of its own and is switched over when the server is ready.

## Dependencies and impact on other modules

- `plans_finished/frontend_app/` reads the archive, the style, the fonts and the sprites. Its plan resumes when FR-1 - FR-6 are met.
- `plans/osm_import/` builds the loading program. The backend persons receive the archive file from Adrian and load it there; `MVP.md` still gives that step to this initiative and is corrected.
- `plans/deployment_config/` and the server part decided in `plans_finished/backend_architecture/` serve the archive from the place the step writes to, answering byte ranges.
- The owner of the server receives the archive file from Adrian by hand. No address, login or secret of the server enters the repository.
- `docs/standards/standard_frontend.md` leaves the choice of FR-4 to this initiative and records it once it is made. `MVP.md` is updated when this initiative finishes, as that file asks.
- The description of the data sources of the Kraków submission takes from here the source of the base map, its day and its licence.

## Risks and notes

- The archive exists as one file on one machine. If it is lost, it has to be cut again from the same build, which depends on that build still being offered.
- The style and a script are code of the frontend, and the gates of the frontend are set up by `plans_finished/frontend_app/`, which runs after this initiative by the decision of Adrian. Until then they are checked by review alone, as `docs/standards/standard_frontend.md` allows.
- Until the backend persons load the archive, the hosted demo has no map of its own; the frontend is tested on its temporary stand-in. The backend persons have not confirmed that they take the loading over.
- Handing the file over is a step of a person outside the repository.
- Few streets of Kraków carry an English name, so switching the language changes few labels. That is the expected result, not a defect.
- Kuber, the second owner in `MVP.md`, has not confirmed the shape of this initiative.
